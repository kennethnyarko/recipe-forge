Tomato Beef Pasta

Description

Tomato beef pasta is a simple and filling dish made with pasta, ground beef, and a rich tomato sauce. It is perfect for a quick lunch or dinner.

Recipe

The pasta is cooked separately and then combined with seasoned ground beef and homemade tomato sauce for a flavorful meal.

Ingredients

  • 400g pasta
  • 300g ground beef
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta until tender and drain it.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until brown.
  5. Add the tomato paste and tomato sauce.
  6. Season with oregano, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Simmer the sauce for 10 to 15 minutes.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  9. Top with Parmesan cheese and serve.
