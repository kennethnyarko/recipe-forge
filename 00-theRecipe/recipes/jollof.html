Jollof

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and spices. It is flavorful and can be served with chicken, beef, fried plantain, or vegetables.

Recipe

This recipe creates delicious and flavorful jollof rice by cooking the rice slowly in a rich tomato and pepper sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ginger
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, onion, garlic, and ginger.
  3. Heat the oil and fry the onions and tomato paste.
  4. Add the blended tomato mixture and cook until thick.
  5. Add the spices, salt, and chicken stock.
  6. Add the rice and stir everything together.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is tender.
  8. Serve hot with chicken, beef, plantain, or vegetables.
