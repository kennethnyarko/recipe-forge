Lasagna

ABOUT

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, seasoned beef, tomato sauce, and melted cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is great for sharing.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic.
  2. Add tomato sauce and seasonings.
  3. Cook the lasagna sheets.
  4. Add meat sauce to a baking dish.
  5. Add lasagna sheets and cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers.
  7. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  8. Bake until golden and bubbly.
  9. Let it cool and serve.
