Sushi

Description

Sushi is a Japanese dish made with seasoned rice, vegetables, and fish wrapped in sheets of nori seaweed. It is light, fresh, and easy to customize with different fillings.

Recipe

This sushi recipe combines seasoned rice with salmon, cucumber, and avocado before rolling everything together in nori seaweed.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 4 sheets nori seaweed
  • 200g salmon
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 avocado
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Soy sauce
  • Wasabi

Steps

  1. Cook the sushi rice and allow it to cool.
  2. Mix the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt into the rice.
  3. Slice the salmon, cucumber, and avocado into thin pieces.
  4. Place a sheet of nori on a sushi mat.
  5. Spread the rice evenly over the nori.
  6. Place the salmon, cucumber, and avocado in the center.
  7. Roll the sushi tightly using the sushi mat.
  8. Cut the roll into small pieces.
  9. Serve with soy sauce and wasabi.
